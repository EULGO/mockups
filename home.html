Who we are

About the Gateway

  • What it is

    The Gateway is a trusted starting point for Indian ICT professionals, students and researchers exploring opportunities in the European Union, and for EU employers and institutions seeking talent from India. It explains legal mobility in plain language, helps people explore potentially relevant pathways, provides practical resources and connects users with authoritative EU and Member State information.

  • Who runs it

    The Gateway is a European Union initiative implemented by the International Centre for Migration Policy Development (ICMPD). It brings together EU-level guidance, verified Member State information and ICMPD’s experience in migration governance to support transparent and well-informed mobility between India and the European Union.

  • Why it exists

    Mobility between India and the EU is governed by 27 separate national systems alongside EU-wide frameworks. Prospective applicants often rely on fragmented or outdated information, and in some cases on misleading recruitment claims; employers and institutions encounter the same complexity from the other direction. The Gateway was established to provide a single trusted reference point and to support an enabling environment for legal, transparent and skills-driven mobility between India and the European Union.
