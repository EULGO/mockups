In brief

ICT specialists were 3.8% of employment in Italy in 2025; demand is moderate to high for selected specialist roles, with 83% of systems-administrator entries difficult to fill. Milan / Lombardy, Rome / Lazio and Turin / Piedmont are the main hubs, and the routes include the EU Blue Card, intra-corporate transfer and the Flussi employed-worker route.

02

ICT mobility at a glance

Market signal: hard-to-fill specialist roles, but not a blanket vacancy market

Excelsior reports difficulty-to-recruit rates of 83% for systems administrators, 80% for data/statistical analysts, 75% for cybersecurity specialists, 67% for software analysts/designers, 65% for programmer technicians and 60% for web technicians in 2025. Experience requirements are high across these occupations. Candidates should target verified roles and employers rather than treat shortage rates as guaranteed sponsorship.

03Balanced candidate assessment

Why consider Italy?

Potential advantages

  • • Verified hard-to-fill ICT specialist roles.
  • Blue Card outside Flussi quota; experience alternative for qualifying ICT managers/specialists.
  • Diversified technology/industrial economy and several major hubs.
  • Established India-Italy mobility corridor and bilateral mobility agreement.
  • Study/research routes can transition toward employment.

Things to consider

  • • ICT specialists are 3.8% of employment versus 5.0% EU average.
  • Strongest opportunities are experience-heavy and selective.
  • Italian materially broadens locally oriented roles.
  • General employed-worker recruitment is quota-managed.
  • Blue Card still requires salary, labour-market and documentation conditions.
  • Indian diaspora statistics are all-sector, not an ICT-demand proxy.

04

India-Italy mobility corridor

Italy already has a large India mobility corridor. At 31 December 2024, 159,618 Indian citizens were legally resident; 16,907 new permits were issued in 2024, with work the largest reason. 5,105 Indian students were enrolled at university level in AY 2023/24. The 2023 bilateral Migration and Mobility Agreement also promotes skilled-worker and academic/scientific mobility. These indicators use different definitions and should not be added together.

01Demand, role families and verified benchmarks

Where are the ICT opportunities?

Italy’s ICT-specialist share was 3.8% of employment in 2025, below the EU average of 5.0%. At the same time, official Excelsior evidence points to substantial recruitment difficulty in specific ICT occupations rather than a uniform market-wide shortage.

The strongest official signals are in programming/software, systems administration, data/AI and cybersecurity. Planned recruitment is highly experience-oriented in many of these groups, and systems/data entries are strongly concentrated in larger employers - useful signals for candidates targeting direct recruitment from India.

Where are the jobs?

Main ICT hubs

  • • Milan / Lombardy: principal corporate and international-employer search hub; Lombardy has Italy’s largest regional labour market.
  • Rome / Lazio: EURES identifies Rome as an important hub for intellectual, scientific and highly specialised profiles.
  • Turin / Piedmont: EURES highlights ICT, advanced manufacturing, automotive, aerospace and logistics, concentrated in Metropolitan Turin.
  • Bologna / Emilia-Romagna: manufacturing, machinery/automotive and university/R&D ecosystem.

Employer landscape

  • • Hiring spans core ICT, telecom, consulting, finance, advanced manufacturing, automotive, logistics and other ICT-intensive employers.
  • Systems/data planned entries are heavily concentrated in 50+ employers (92.2%/90.7%), making larger employers a practical target from India.
  • Excelsior measures demand - not employer willingness to sponsor a third-country national.

01Which pathway could apply?

Moving to Italy for work

Indian ICT candidates should compare the EU Blue Card first where the role is highly qualified, then assess intra-company transfer, other Article 27 outside-quota categories, the general Flussi route or researcher status. The Blue Card is especially relevant because qualifying ICT managers/specialists may use three years of relevant experience acquired in the previous seven years instead of a conventional tertiary qualification.

02

From job offer to arrival

  1. Target a suitable ICT role
  2. Confirm route, salary and qualification/experience proof
  3. Employer completes labour-market/authorisation steps where required
  4. Prepare national visa documents in India
  5. Enter Italy and complete stay/residence formalities
  6. Obtain residence permit and local registrations

For the Blue Card, the employer submits the highly qualified work application and, where required, completes the labour-market check through the Centro per l’Impiego. Current national guidance provides for a positive work-authorisation opinion within 30 days; this does not represent the full visa/residence timeline. Since 19 June 2026, Blue Card soggiorno contracts can also be signed and submitted electronically through the Portale Servizi.

04What official 2025 demand data suggest

Applying from India

  • Target specialist profiles first. Systems, data, cybersecurity and advanced software roles show the strongest verified shortage signals; broad junior/generalist applications face more competition.
  • Prove recent experience and operating level. Across the six Excelsior ICT groups highlighted here, only a small minority of planned entries were marked “no experience required”. Show production ownership, scale, security, reliability and measurable impact.
  • Assess employer readiness separately from market demand. Larger firms dominate systems/data planned entries, but Excelsior does not measure immigration sponsorship. Confirm third-country hiring capability, relocation support and route before investing heavily.
  • Make language readiness visible. English matters in technology, but Italian can materially widen the employer set and support long-term integration.
  • Prepare documents early: passport, degree/transcripts, employment contracts or payslips/references for experience-based routes, and apostilled/legalised/translated documents where the chosen process requires them.

Evidence basis: official Excelsior, EURES, national/EU migration and institutional desk research.

05

Recruitment: know your rights

India application point

After employer authorisation, use the official Visa for Italy service and the Italian Embassy/competent Consulate in India. Work authorisation and consular visa issuance are separate stages.

Before accepting an offer

  • • Verify employer/legal entity, work location, gross annual salary (RAL), hours and contract duration.
  • Confirm applicable CCNL/classification and which immigration route will be used.
  • Verify salary, labour-market and document conditions against current official guidance.
  • Get relocation, family and probation/notice terms in writing.
  • Keep copies of contracts, authorisations and receipts.

Red flags

  • • Guaranteed Blue Card/visa in exchange for payment.
  • Money requested for an unverifiable vacancy or authorisation.
  • Salary, role or location differs between recruitment messages and official documents.
  • You are told to start before your lawful work right is valid.
  • A general Flussi quota is presented as a guaranteed ICT visa.
  • Pressure to hand original documents to an unverified intermediary.

Practical information

Before you move

  • • Compare gross annual salary (RAL), expected net pay, housing and relocation package.

03

From study to employment

  1. Complete eligible BA/MA in Italy
  2. Build local experience, language and networks
  3. Apply for one-year job-search status if eligible
  4. Secure qualified ICT offer and confirm route
  5. Convert to appropriate work / Blue Card status

Important post-study distinction

Eligible graduates of Italian higher-education programmes may apply to convert their study permit to a one-year post-study job-search permit (attesa occupazione), subject to the national requirements and application procedure. Before accepting a job, confirm whether the current status allows the proposed activity or needs conversion. A longer-term employed-worker permit or EU Blue Card is subject to its own employment, salary and qualification rules.The job-search permit creates time to look for employment after an eligible Italian degree; it does not guarantee a job or a Blue Card. The eventual work status must meet its own job, salary, qualification/experience and employer-process conditions.

04

Research opportunities

Italy’s research route is relevant for AI, cybersecurity, advanced computing and digital systems across universities and recognised research bodies. Researchers enter on the basis of a hosting agreement with a selected research institution; the route is outside the ordinary Flussi quota.

Find opportunities

  • • EURAXESS Italy / Europe.
  • University/research institute career and doctoral calls.
  • Company R&D and advanced-technology laboratories.
  • Italy-India scientific cooperation: the 2025-2027 bilateral programme includes ICT and AI among mobility fields.

For researchers & doctoral candidates

  • • Hosting agreement required with a selected research body.
  • Apply for research residence permit within 8 days.
  • Family reunification allowed irrespective of permit duration.
  • After research permit expiry, a 9-12 month job-search/start-company permit may be available.
  • Academic fit, supervisor availability and funding remain decisive.

Frequently asked questions

Recognition of Indian qualifications, working in English and moving with your family in Italy.

For ICT professionals

  • • Foreign qualifications do not need formal legal recognition merely to enter a non-regulated profession in Italy.
  • Mainstream software, cloud and cybersecurity roles are generally non-regulated, but candidates should verify the specific occupation.
  • Blue Card rules/employers can still require evidence of degree level or qualifying experience.
  • Regulated professions require recognition by the competent authority.

Cimea / document evidence

  • • CIMEA is Italy’s ENIC/NARIC reference centre for qualification information/evaluation services.
  • Blue Card qualification documents must be authenticated/legalised/apostilled as applicable and translated into Italian; current guidance allows Declaration of Value or CIMEA comparability/authenticity for higher-education titles.
  • Experience-based Blue Card claims are supported by contracts and/or payslips; employer letter optional.
  • Qualification evaluation does not itself confer work or residence rights.

Blue Card, ICT and researcher routes include facilitated family provisions

Blue Card holders may reunite with family regardless of permit duration. ICT transferees may likewise be accompanied/reunited regardless of permit duration and the family residence permit includes the right to work. Researcher family reunification is also allowed irrespective of permit duration. Other work statuses follow their specific/general family rules.
