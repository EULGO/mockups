In brief

Latvia is a compact ICT market (4.5% of employment in 2025, 7,355 ICT enterprises) that offers selective opportunities for experienced specialists rather than a blanket shortage. Riga is the dominant technology hub, 3,034 Indian nationals were granted employment rights in 2025, and the EU Blue Card is the main route for highly qualified roles.

Market signal: economically important sector, selective current hiring

Latvia’s ICT sector accounts for 7.1% of GDP in the latest LIKTA compilation and is a major exporter. However, ICT specialists represent 4.5% of employment, below the EU average, and the mapped vacancies are concentrated in experienced profiles. Candidates should assess specific employers and live vacancies rather than infer universal demand.

03Balanced candidate assessment

Why consider Latvia?

Potential advantages

  • Compact, internationally connected technology ecosystem with strong software and IT-service orientation.
  • Visible strengths in fintech, telecoms, electronics, cloud, data/AI and language technologies.
  • Riga hosts international employers, start-ups, universities and business-service centres.
  • English is commonly used in internationally oriented teams.
  • Blue Card thresholds are transparent and the shortage threshold is relatively accessible for qualifying roles.
  • Established Indian higher-education and employment presence.

Things to consider

  • Small absolute market and strong concentration in Riga; vacancy volumes can be limited.
  • Active mapped demand is concentrated in mid-level and senior profiles rather than juniors.
  • ICT-specialist share is below the EU average despite the sector’s economic importance.
  • Latvian can be important outside international teams and for long-term integration.
  • Salaries are lower than in many western/northern EU hubs, although living costs are generally lower.
  • One employer consultation raised screening and integration concerns; candidates should not assume employer readiness for direct relocation.

01Demand, role families and verified benchmarks

Where are the ICT opportunities?

Latvia has a compact, export-oriented ICT ecosystem centred overwhelmingly in Riga. The sector combines software and IT services, telecommunications, fintech, electronics, cloud and data activity. ICT specialists represented 4.5% of employment in 2025, below the EU average of 5.0%, while sector output and exports remain economically significant. Official data show that information and communication value added grew by 4.9% in 2025, led by computer programming and consultancy.

The internal EULGO Latvia mapping provides a demand-side view across 73 employer records representing 72 unique companies. Fifty-two records showed active priority ICT roles. Software engineering was the clearest signal, followed by data/AI, cloud/DevOps, systems/network operations, product/UX/architecture, QA and cybersecurity. Demand was strongly tilted toward mid-level and senior specialists. These are directional observations from named employers, not a representative national vacancy survey.

01Which pathway could apply?

Moving to Latvia for work

For highly qualified Indian ICT professionals, the EU Blue Card is normally the most relevant route. Latvia also has an ordinary employed-worker route, intra-corporate transfer provisions and a researcher route. Eligibility depends on the contract, occupation, salary, qualification or professional experience, and employer-side invitation procedure.

04What the Latvia mapping suggests

Applying from India

  • Target specialised roles where international recruitment is most plausible. The mapping shows the strongest signals in backend/full-stack development, data engineering and AI, cloud/DevOps/SRE, systems infrastructure, QA automation and selected security roles.
  • Demonstrate recent, production-level experience. Most active employer records sought mid-level or senior specialists. Applications should show scale, ownership, reliability, security, measurable outcomes and the exact technologies used—not only broad skill labels.
  • Address relocation and integration readiness directly. One regional employer consultation raised concerns about candidate screening at scale and integration of larger relocated groups. This is qualitative evidence from one employer, not a general judgement about Indian candidates, but it shows why credible assessment and preparation matter.
  • Make language readiness visible. English is common in international technology, fintech, shared-service and product teams. Latvian can still be important for local customers, public-sector work, smaller firms and long-term integration.
  • Prepare the mobility file early. Keep your passport, degree and transcript, detailed employment references, criminal-record certificate where required, insurance and civil-status documents ready. Confirm whether documents need legalisation, translation or academic assessment for the selected route.

05

Recruitment: know your rights

India application point

Applicants eligible to lodge a residence-permit application in India use the Embassy of Latvia in New Delhi and its official appointment procedure. The Embassy directs applicants with an OCMA decision or employment-visa need to the designated VFS channel. Always follow the route-specific Embassy and OCMA instructions; do not rely on commercial agents for legal eligibility.

Before accepting an offer

  • Verify the employer, legal entity, work location, gross salary, probation, working time and benefits in writing.
  • Confirm the exact residence route and which employer-side vacancy/invitation steps are required.
  • Check whether the role qualifies for the reduced Blue Card threshold or must meet the general threshold.
  • Clarify who pays application, translation, insurance, travel and relocation costs.

Red flags

  • A recruiter guarantees approval of a visa or residence permit.
  • Payment is requested for a non-verifiable vacancy, invitation or contract.
  • The employer, salary, location or role differs between the offer and official documents.
  • You are told to start work before the correct right to employment is valid.
  • Your passport or original documents are retained without a lawful reason and receipt.

Practical information

Before you move

  • Compare the gross offer with Riga living costs and expected net income; sector averages are not guarantees.
  • Confirm relocation, temporary housing, family support, start-date flexibility and probation conditions.
  • Ask which language is used internally and with customers; begin Latvian learning if relocating long-term.
  • Prepare translations, insurance, accommodation and police-clearance evidence required by the route.
  • Use only the Embassy, OCMA and designated submission channel for India-specific instructions.

On arrival

  • Collect the residence permit and declare the place of residence as required.
  • Complete employer payroll/social-insurance registration and arrange health coverage.
  • Provide any route-specific medical/radiology documentation required after arrival.
  • Open banking and obtain the personal identity/digital credentials needed for services.
  • Notify OCMA of changes to employer, profession, address or family circumstances where required.

02

Higher education options & costs

Selected ICT-relevant institutions

  • Riga Technical University: computer systems, telecoms/data transmission, smart electronics, cybersecurity and doctoral ICT programmes.
  • University of Latvia: computer science, data science, information systems, cybersecurity and doctoral study.
  • Transport and Telecommunication Institute: computer science, engineering/electronics, robotics and information systems.
  • Vidzeme University of Applied Sciences: IT, cybersecurity, virtual reality and socio-technical systems.
  • Other mapped providers include LBTU, Ventspils UAS, RISEBA, Turība, EKA and regional/VET providers.

Study conditions & costs

  • Many mapped university-level ICT programmes are English-taught or bilingual; most mapped VET provision is Latvian-language.
  • Study in Latvia reports average tuition across fields of approximately EUR 2,800–15,000 per year; verify the specific ICT programme.
  • Working during studies: Full-time bachelor's students: up to 20 hours/week, or 40 during holidays. Full-time master's/doctoral students: no statutory work-hour restriction under current Study in Latvia guidance.
  • Financial-proof requirements are route-specific and should be verified with OCMA for the application date.
  • Scholarships are available but do not necessarily cover full tuition and living costs.

The institution list reflects the internal EULGO academic mapping and is illustrative rather than a ranking or complete programme catalogue. Programme language, tuition, accreditation and admissions requirements should be confirmed directly with the institution and Study in Latvia.

From study to employment

  1. Complete eligible studies in Latvia
  2. Use career services and target employers early
  3. Secure a qualified job offer
  4. Confirm Blue Card or other work route
  5. Change status before starting the job

Critical distinction for Indian graduates

Study in Latvia states that the study residence permit normally includes four additional months after graduation. Eligible full-time master's and doctoral graduates can request a further nine-month job-search extension before the existing permit expires; OCMA assesses each application. This post-study period does not itself authorise employment. Confirm the applicable route and obtain employment-authorising status before starting work. Bachelor's graduates should confirm their options directly with OCMA.

04

Research opportunities

Latvia’s research ecosystem includes computer science, language technologies, AI, telecommunications, electronics, robotics and cybersecurity. The University of Latvia, Riga Technical University, the Institute of Electronics and Computer Science and the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Science are among the most relevant actors. EURAXESS should be used for current positions and funding opportunities.

Find opportunities

  • EURAXESS Latvia / EURAXESS Europe.
  • University of Latvia institutes and doctoral programmes.
  • Riga Technical University research groups and doctoral studies.
  • Institute of Electronics and Computer Science.
  • Institute of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Latvia.
  • Latvian Council of Science calls and corporate R&D opportunities.

For researchers & doctoral candidates

  • Researchers need an eligible agreement with a registered scientific institution.
  • The researcher route provides unrestricted labour access for the permitted research activity.
  • A researcher residence permit may be issued for up to five years, depending on the agreement.
  • Family reunification is possible and family employment rights should be confirmed for the issued status.
  • After research, an eligible researcher may request up to nine months to seek work or establish a business.

Frequently asked questions

Recognition of Indian qualifications, working in English and moving with your family in Latvia.

For ICT professionals

  • Most mainstream software, data, cloud, cyber and IT roles are not regulated professions; employers assess technical suitability.
  • Immigration eligibility can still require evidence of a qualifying degree or professional experience.
  • For the Blue Card, current official guidance accepts a 3+ year higher-education programme or at least 5 years’ relevant professional experience.
  • Keep detailed employer letters stating dates, duties, technologies, seniority and full-time status.

Academic recognition / documents

  • The Academic Information Centre (Latvian ENIC/NARIC) evaluates foreign diplomas for academic recognition in Latvia.
  • Regulated professions require recognition by the relevant competent authority; most ICT roles are non-regulated.
  • Universities assess prior qualifications for admission and may request ENIC/NARIC input.
  • Confirm whether Indian documents require translation, legalisation or additional verification for the purpose concerned.

English is common in international ICT—but Latvian still matters

International technology, fintech, telecom, start-up and global-business-services teams frequently work in English. The mapping shows many English or bilingual university programmes. Latvian can nevertheless be required for local clients, public-sector interaction, operational roles, smaller companies and many vocational programmes. Candidates should verify the actual team language and show willingness to learn.

Family mobility depends on the principal route

Family members can apply to join eligible workers, Blue Card holders, researchers and other qualifying permit holders. The EU Immigration Portal states that a family member of a third-country citizen holding a temporary residence permit based on employment or self-employment has unrestricted access to work in Latvia. Conditions, documents and timing depend on the sponsor’s exact status.
