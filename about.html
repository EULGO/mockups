About the European Legal Gateway Office in India

  • What it is

    The European Legal Gateway Office in India is a one-stop hub providing Indian ICT students, researchers and professionals with clear, reliable information on legal mobility opportunities across the European Union. It brings together information on EU and Member State opportunities, pathways and requirements, making it easier for Indian talent to explore options and navigate the steps involved in moving to Europe for work, study or research.

    The European Legal Gateway Office also supports European employers and higher education institutions seeking to engage with Indian ICT talent by providing information on the Indian skills and education landscape and facilitating connections with relevant stakeholders.

    By bringing information and opportunities together in one place, it simplifies access, strengthens the visibility and reach of existing mobility pathways and provides a coordinated entry point for India–EU ICT mobility.

  • Who runs it

    Launched in February 2026, the European Legal Gateway Office in India is funded by the European Union under the framework of Migration Partnership Facility and implemented by the International Centre for Migration Policy Development (ICMPD). ICMPD is an international organisation with over three decades of experience in migration governance, supporting governments, EU institutions, UN agencies, and other partners in more than 90 countries through policy expertise, research and capacity development.

    The Gateway operates through three interconnected components: an EU Gateway Office in India, an EU Support Office in Brussels, and an EU-branded digital tool that connects candidates, employers and higher education institutions with relevant information and opportunities.

  • How it works

    The Gateway helps you in four steps:

    • Understand: learn the basic concepts before answering pathway questions.
    • Explore: see potentially relevant legal pathways based on structured criteria.
    • Prepare: use guides, checklists and procedural information.
    • Continue: go to the responsible EU or Member State authority.

    Guidance and signposting, not legal advice. Pathway information is linked to official EU and Member State sources.

  • Mission

    Mobility pathways between the EU and India have historically been developed through Member State-level initiatives, resulting in a landscape that has been complex for Indian talent to navigate and has not reached its full potential in terms of visibility and impact. The European Legal Gateway Office in India was established to address this by offering a cohesive, EU-branded platform that amplifies visibility, simplifies access to trusted information, and enables structured cooperation with Indian institutions.

    The EU legal gateway office positions and brands the EU as a globally visible and attractive destination for skilled talent, starting with India’s ICT sector. It enhances the visibility of mobility pathways and opportunities offered by the EU and its Member States in India, expanding awareness and access to legal pathways for Indian ICT professionals, students and researchers seeking to work, study or conduct research in Europe.
