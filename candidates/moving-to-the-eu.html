1Understand

There are four legal ways to move to Europe.

Most Indian professionals, students and researchers use one of them. Every country runs its own version, so the details differ, but the shape is the same.

Work

With a job offer, or while looking for one.

  • EU Blue Card
  • National permits
  • Job-seeker visas
Working in the EU
Study

With a place at a university or college.

  • Student visas
  • Erasmus Mundus
Studying in the EU
Research

With a hosting agreement from an institute.

  • Researcher permits
  • Marie Skłodowska-Curie
Research in the EU
Family

Joining someone who already lives there.

  • Family reunification
  • Blue Card families
Joining family
25 EU countries issue the EU Blue Card: all except Denmark and Ireland.

      Before you pay anyone

      Three things to remember

      1. Nobody can guarantee a visa. If anyone asks for money to “guarantee” one, stop.
      2. Check the address. The EU’s own websites end in europa.eu. A site that asks you to pay to apply “through” it is not an official portal.
      3. The Gateway never takes applications or payments. It is free, and it never asks for your phone number.
